Hey everyone!
These are a model of Yeezy slides! 
I created them for Half Life by Hack Club.
I wanted to design these as I have seen a lot of people with slides like this and I wanted to do something that was easier for a beginner.
I started with Nike Minds, but I realized that these were WAY too complicated and decided to do Yeezys instead.
I used reference images and traced the basic details of the shoe. I then extruded the topviews and sideviews and used the intersection tool to merge them and make a half-decent shoe model.
I had to restart a couple times, which makes my hours seem really inflated. I did actually spend time and design models during those times, and they aren't just filler hours.
Whoever reviews my project, thank you so much! :D
